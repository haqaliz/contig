# Outreach batch 2 — contact-form and community routes

**Status 2026-11-11.** Email routes are sent and logged in
`outreach_targets.csv`. The routes below need an interactive browser or
account; send the message as written and move the tracker row to `sent` only
after submission.

Research-use only. No PHI, no patient data, no clinical claims. Use the
organization's public form; do not scrape personal emails.

---

## 1. Broad Institute Genomics Platform

**Route:** https://www.broadinstitute.org/contact (ask for the Genomics
Platform / Broad Clinical Labs)

**Subject:** One panel/WES run for a research-use coverage-readiness check?

> Hi — I maintain Contig, a local research-use workflow that checks coverage
> readiness and builds an HTML report with hashed provenance. I am validating
> it on real panel/WES data and looking for one run with the coverage you would
> expect, plus your ready thresholds if you have them. Nothing
> patient-identifying leaves your lab; I can sign a redaction/data-use
> agreement first. In return I will send a short summary of how the readiness
> states compare to your expectations. Could you forward this to the Genomics
> Platform team?

## 7. nf-core Slack

**Route:** https://nf-co.re/join — join, set the profile to `Ali (Contig)`,
then post once in `#help` (not every channel):

> Hi all — I maintain Contig, a local research-use layer on top of nf-core
> outputs: a filtered variant table, a coverage/readiness table, and an HTML
> report with hashed provenance. It never sends raw reads and makes no clinical
> claims. I am looking for 3–5 people who would spend 60 minutes running it on
> their own VCF/panel/BED (or a sarek/rnaseq run) and telling me where the
> defaults are wrong. Happy to share the report and a short summary.
> Issue form: https://github.com/haqaliz/contig/issues/new?template=design_partner.yml

## 8. Biostars

`variant-calling`, `coverage`, `report`, `validation`.

> I maintain Contig (open source, local-first). It takes a VCF plus a panel/BED
> through a deterministic filtered variant table, a coverage/readiness table,
> and an HTML report with hashed provenance. It does not send raw reads and
> makes no clinical claims. I am validating the defaults on real lab data and
> looking for 3–5 people willing to try it on a dataset they already have for 60
> minutes. The goal is to find where the filters/thresholds/report wording are
> wrong, not to sell anything. Details and the intake form:
> https://github.com/haqaliz/contig/issues/new?template=design_partner.yml
